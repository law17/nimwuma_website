## Shared Operations

Choose a defined amount of managed human delivery capacity each month. This works well for businesses that need recurring support with administration, CRM updates, inbox work, document processing, appointment scheduling, customer follow-ups, research or order administration but do not need a dedicated full-time professional.

## Dedicated Operations Assistant

A trained professional works primarily on your business while remaining supported by {{ site.data.company.brand.name }}'s management, systems, quality assurance and process documentation.

## Dedicated Customer Support

Managed support for agreed channels such as email, chat and appropriate phone workflows during defined service hours. Service levels, escalation rules and client-specific knowledge are established during onboarding.

## Managed Operations Team

For businesses requiring several people, {{ site.data.company.brand.name }} can structure a managed team with supervision, performance reporting, quality assurance and process ownership.

Standard global pricing is expressed in USD. Alternative settlement currencies may be agreed for suitable contracts, but the canonical international price book remains USD-based.
