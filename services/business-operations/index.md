### Business Essentials

Designed for genuinely small businesses with modest administrative volumes. It can cover essential sales and expense records, invoicing administration, receivables tracking, customer and supplier records, document organisation and a monthly business summary.

### Business Operations

Our core SME package. It adds broader operational support such as payroll preparation, CRM administration, more active receivables management, compliance tracking, basic inventory administration and management reporting.

### Managed Office

Capacity-based support for businesses that need ongoing administration during the working week. Depending on the selected capacity, it can include shared inbox administration, customer enquiries, CRM management, operational coordination, broader payroll administration and enhanced reporting.

## Important service boundaries

{{ site.data.company.brand.name }} does not ordinarily take possession of client funds or make sensitive payments on a client's behalf. Clients retain financial authority. Statutory audit, legal advice and specialist tax advice are outside the standard managed-operations scope and should be handled by appropriately qualified professionals.

## Analytics included where appropriate

Business activity can be turned into KPIs, charts, management summaries, alerts and recommended attention. The aim is not only to complete operational work, but also to help owners understand what the work is revealing about the business.
