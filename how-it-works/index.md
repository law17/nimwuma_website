## 1. Understand

We identify the business processes being delegated, the required inputs, decision boundaries, service expectations and relevant risks.

## 2. Configure

We establish agreed workflows, access, permissions, standard operating procedures, reporting and escalation rules.

## 3. Operate

{{ site.data.company.brand.name }} performs the agreed business processes using trained people and appropriate automation.

## 4. Measure

Operational activity, workload and service performance are recorded so delivery can be understood rather than guessed.

## 5. Analyse

Relevant data can be transformed into KPIs, charts, trends, exceptions and management insight.

## 6. Improve

Repeated processes are reviewed for simplification, better controls and automation where this improves quality or efficiency.

### AI prepares. Humans review. Clients retain authority.

AI and automation can support repetitive work and analysis. Sensitive decisions remain subject to the agreed human and client controls.
